How FoodBridge works

Three steps to connect surplus food with people who need it.

01
🏪

Donors add food

Hotels, restaurants, hostels and marriage halls list surplus food, quantity and pickup time.

02
🤝

NGOs claim food

Registered NGOs browse available donations and claim suitable listings for collection.

03
🍲

Food gets collected

The NGO reaches the donor location and updates the collection status.

ℹ️
Food safety matters

Donors should provide accurate food details and safe pickup times. NGOs should verify food condition before distribution.
